How it works

From gig to payday in three steps

No chasing invoices. No ghosted payments. Every naira accounted for.

Client

Post a gig with milestones

Describe the job, set a price, break it into milestones, and pay the initial deposit into escrow. Your money is locked — not spent — until work is delivered.

Freelancer

Claim it and deliver

Browse open gigs in The Space, claim one that fits your skills, and start the clock. Mark each milestone delivered as you go.

Both sides

Admin approves, wallet gets credited

When the client is satisfied and requests release, admin confirms and the freelancer's wallet is credited. Clean, transparent, no arguments.

Escrow protection

Your money doesn't move until you say so

Every gig on Provaspace runs through our escrow system. Clients pay upfront; freelancers know the money is real; releases only happen with admin sign-off.

🔒

Deposit held at gig post

Clients deposit funds when posting — no freelancer starts work on a promise.

⚖️

Admin-gated milestone release

Every payment release is reviewed and approved by a human admin before it hits a wallet.

🛡️

Dispute protection built in

If something goes wrong, either side can raise a dispute. Funds stay locked until admin resolves it.
