Tax Pass

Clients

Tax Pass is what a client buys to unlock the ability to post gigs on Provaspace. Without it, you can browse the platform but you can't put work out into the Space. Think of it as your client-side operating licence.

It's not a per-gig charge — you buy a Tax Pass once per tier and it covers a set number of gig posts. When you run out, you buy again.

WHAT IT COVERS

Each tier gives you a fixed number of gig slots. Higher tier = more gigs you can post before renewing.

PRICING

Tiers and prices are set by the admin — check the Tax Pass card on your client dashboard for the current options.

💡
FREE TIERIf admin sets a tier price to ₦0, it shows as Free and activates instantly — no payment screen, no Paystack.
The Post Gig button goes inactive and the form won't submit. Buy a new Tax Pass from your client dashboard to top up your slots.
No. Each purchase is a fresh set of slots. Unused slots from a previous tier don't roll over when you buy again.

Rent

Freelancers

Rent is the freelancer's recurring subscription to the Space. It keeps your profile active and your ability to claim gigs alive. No active rent = no access to The Space.

You pick a plan that suits how often you work — weekly, monthly, or yearly. Yearly is the cheapest per day; weekly is the most flexible if you're testing the waters.

PLANS

Weekly, Monthly, Yearly — prices set by admin. Check your dashboard Rent card for current rates.

GRACE PERIOD

When rent expires, you get a grace period before you're blocked. After that, an overdue fee kicks in and your account is suspended until you pay.

🎁
FREE RENT VIA REFERRALSRefer someone to the Space. Once they complete their first gig, you earn free rent days — credited directly to your rent due date. No cash, just extra time in the Space.
⚠️
NEW USERSIf you're brand new and haven't been referred by anyone, you must pay rent before you can access the dashboard fully. There's no free trial unless you have referral credit.
You get a grace period first (set by admin — typically a few days). During that window you can still access everything. After grace period ends, an overdue fee is added and your account is suspended. Pay off the balance to get back in.
Yes — when your current rent period expires, just pick a new plan from the Rent card on your dashboard.
You still pay rent eventually, but your referral credit gives you free days first. The days are applied to your rent due date automatically once you're credited.

Silver Surfer

It can answer questions about the platform, help you figure out how things work, and if it can't solve your problem, it logs a support ticket automatically so admin can follow up. You get both the assistant and a ticket in one tap.

The Silver Surfer button — bottom-right corner of your dashboard.

Chat with Silver Surfer and your support ticket is created at the same time.

ON CONTRACT PAGESThe Silver Surfer button sits at z-index above the chat input on contract pages so it doesn't block the send button — if it ever does, try scrolling the page slightly.

Insurance & Assurance

These are two separate but related things — insurance is what a client adds to a gig when posting it, and assurance is what happens when something goes wrong on any contract.

INSURANCE (CLIENT SIDE)

When posting a gig, a client can opt into insurance. This adds a fee on top of the deposit — held by the platform as extra protection for the job.

ASSURANCE (FREELANCER SIDE)

When a freelancer claims an insured gig, they pay a flat assurance fee before the contract is created. This fee is set by admin and held for the contract duration.

🛡️
WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU CLAIM AN INSURED GIGAs soon as you tap Claim, a toast notification appears telling you the gig is insured. A modal then shows the exact assurance fee. You must pay it via Paystack before the contract is created. If you close the payment screen, nothing is charged and no contract is made.
Partially — if the contract completes without a dispute, the fee is considered absorbed. If a dispute is raised and resolved in your favour, admin may factor the assurance fee into the resolution.
Yes — insured gigs show a shield icon on their listing card. The gig preview modal also shows the assurance fee amount before you commit.

Trust Score

Trust Score is a number between 0 and 100 that shows how reliable you've been on the platform. Every account starts at 100. It goes down when things go wrong — and admin can adjust it directly.

WHAT LOWERS IT

Abandoning an active contract deducts 15 points. Disputes resolved against you may also lead to a reduction. Admin can also manually adjust your score.

HOW IT'S USED

Gigs in The Space are sorted by Trust Score — higher scores show up higher. Clients browsing freelancer profiles can see your score too.

80 – 100 · Excellent 50 – 79 · Watch out 0 – 49 · Flagged
🔴
BELOW 50Accounts with a Trust Score under 50 are visible to admin in a dedicated flagged-users panel. Admin can see when your score was last reduced and can set it to any value they choose.
Only admin can raise your Trust Score. If you believe your score was reduced unfairly, raise a support ticket via Silver Surfer and explain your case.
15 points are deducted immediately. The gig returns to The Space so another freelancer can pick it up. Your client is notified. The platform tracks how far you got (which milestones were already paid) so the next freelancer doesn't start from scratch.

Privacy Policy

Provaspace doesn't use your data for anything shady. The information you provide — your name, email, NIN, bank details, and gig history — is used only to operate the platform. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to profile you outside of Provaspace.

Your bank details are stored to process withdrawal requests only. NIN details are submitted for admin verification and not exposed to other users. Your wallet balance and contract history are visible only to you and to admins.

🔒
If you have a specific privacy concern, raise a support ticket via Silver Surfer and an admin will respond directly.

What Gets You Banned

A ban is the most serious action on the platform. It locks you out with a blocking overlay the moment you open your dashboard — you can't access anything until admin lifts it.

Too many disputes raised against you Fraud or payment abuse Harassment of other users Admin discretion

Bans are applied by admin, not automatically by the system. If you believe you've been banned unfairly, contact support — but note that appeals aren't guaranteed.

🚫
DIFFERENT FROM SUSPENSIONA ban is not the same as a suspension. Suspension is for rent issues and can be cleared by paying. A ban requires admin to manually restore your account.

What Gets You Suspended

Suspension on Provaspace is almost always about rent. If you let your rent lapse past the grace period without paying, your account gets suspended. Your profile is still there — you just can't claim gigs or access the Space until the balance is cleared.

HOW TO FIX IT

Go to your dashboard, open the Rent card, and pay what's owed — including any overdue fee admin has set. Your access is restored immediately once payment goes through.

GRACE PERIOD

You get a buffer after your rent expires before suspension kicks in. Use that window to renew — the overdue fee only applies after the grace period ends.

Withdrawals & Minimum

When a client releases a milestone payment, it lands in your Provaspace wallet. From there you can request a withdrawal to your saved bank account.

  1. Make sure your bank name, account number, and account name are saved on your profile.
  2. Go to your dashboard and tap Withdraw on your wallet card.
  3. Enter the amount — up to your full wallet balance.
  4. Confirm. Your request goes to admin for review and is paid out via bank transfer.
⏳
PENDING BALANCEOnce you submit a withdrawal request, that amount moves to Pending and is deducted from your available balance. It stays pending until admin approves and processes the bank transfer.
💡
MINIMUM AMOUNTThe minimum withdrawal is set by admin and can change over time as the platform grows. Check your dashboard withdrawal modal for the current minimum.
Withdrawals are processed manually by admin. Timing depends on the admin's processing schedule — if it's taking long, raise a support ticket via Silver Surfer.
Withdrawals go to whatever bank details are saved on your profile at the time of the request. Update your profile first if you need to change it.

Milestones & Escrow

When a client posts a gig, they break it into milestones — named stages of the work, each with a percentage of the total payment attached. Milestones are how money moves from client to freelancer.

HOW MILESTONES WORK

Freelancer marks a milestone as delivered. Client reviews the work and taps Release Payment. The money goes to the freelancer's wallet instantly.

WHAT IS ESCROW

Escrow is money held by the platform on behalf of both parties. The client's deposit goes into escrow when the gig is posted. Milestone payments are released from escrow — not directly from the client's bank each time.

💰
DEPOSIT → FINAL PAYMENTClients pay a deposit when posting a gig. This goes into escrow. As milestones are released and escrow runs low, the client must top up to cover remaining milestones. The contract detail page shows a "Pay" button on the contract card when a top-up is needed.
✅
NO ADMIN APPROVAL NEEDEDClients release payments directly — no waiting for admin to approve. Once you tap Release Payment, the money hits the freelancer's wallet immediately.
Raise a dispute from the Disputes page. Admin reviews it and can release funds from escrow if your work is found to be complete.
Exactly 100%. The form won't let you submit until the total is 100%. The counter turns green when you're there.
No — each milestone releases its full percentage of the total price in one go. Partial releases aren't supported.

Contracts & How Jobs Work

A contract is created the moment a freelancer claims a gig (or a client directly assigns one). It's the living record of the job — milestones, payments, messages, and status all in one place.

  1. Client posts a gig — sets title, description, price, milestones, deposit, and optional insurance.
  2. Freelancer claims it — from The Space (or is directly assigned). A contract is created immediately.
  3. Work happens — both sides communicate via the in-contract chat. Freelancer marks milestones as delivered.
  4. Client releases payment — taps Release Payment on each delivered milestone. Money goes to freelancer's wallet.
  5. Contract completes — when all milestones are released, the contract is marked complete.
💬
CONTRACT CHATEach contract has its own private chat between client and freelancer. You can send text and images. On mobile, the input bar always stays at the bottom of the screen — above Silver Surfer — so the send button is never blocked.
Yes — both the freelancer dashboard and the client dashboard have an Active Contracts section. Every contract you're part of shows up there, including ones where a client hired you directly.
Your Trust Score drops by 15 points, the client is notified, and the gig goes back to The Space for another freelancer to claim. That next freelancer can see which milestones were already paid — they won't be asked to redo paid work.

Disputes

If something goes wrong on a contract — client won't pay, freelancer disappeared, work was unsatisfactory — either side can raise a dispute. Admin reviews it and decides on a resolution.

HOW TO RAISE ONE

Go to the Disputes page from your dashboard sidebar. Click Raise a Dispute, select the contract it's about, describe the problem. It's submitted to admin immediately.

WHAT ADMIN DOES

Admin reviews both sides, looks at contract history, chat logs, and escrow balance, then writes a resolution. Funds held in escrow can be redistributed based on the outcome.

🔴
ABUSE WARNINGRaising false disputes is grounds for suspension or a ban. The dispute system exists to protect both parties honestly — don't weaponise it.
You can raise a dispute on any contract you're part of — active or past. Select it from the dropdown when submitting.
It's admin-reviewed, so timing varies. If your dispute has been sitting for more than a few days without a response, follow up via Silver Surfer.

Direct Hire

Clients

Instead of posting a gig to The Space for any freelancer to claim, you can assign it directly to a specific freelancer. This bypasses the open marketplace entirely — the contract is created immediately and shows up in both your dashboards as active.

  1. Go to Post Gig and fill in all the usual details — price, milestones, deposit, etc.
  2. At the bottom of the form, enter the freelancer's Provaspace email in the Assign to a specific freelancer field.
  3. Wait for the lookup to confirm their account (it searches as you type — green tick means found).
  4. Submit. Payment goes through Paystack, the gig is created as claimed, and the contract goes live. The freelancer gets a notification.
  5. You can also achieve this easily by going to the space as a client and finding the specific feelancer, click assign gig on his/her card and the email field in post-gig automatically updates
✅
SHOWS IN ACTIVE CONTRACTSDirect hire contracts appear in the client's Active Contracts list straight away — no waiting for a freelancer to claim. The freelancer also sees it immediately on their dashboard.
💡
Leave the email field blank to post to The Space as normal. Direct hire is purely optional.
