1

Tax Pass

Clients

Tax Pass is what a client pays to get full access to the Space — posting gigs, messaging freelancers, and running jobs through the platform. It's your client-side membership, not a per-gig fee.

2

Rent

Freelancers

Rent is the freelancer's side of the same idea — a recurring fee that keeps your profile active and eligible to claim gigs in the Space. Pay it weekly, monthly, or yearly, whichever suits how you work.

3

Silver Surfer

Silver Surfer is the Space's built-in support assistant — always on, always ready to help you figure something out.

How to access it: on your dashboard, look to the bottom right — you'll see a bles button there. Click it and you're in: you get Silver Surfer plus a support ticket, so if the assistant can't sort your issue, it's already logged and waiting on an admin.

4

Insurance & Assurance

Freelancers

Insurance is an optional fee freelancers can opt into for extra protection on a gig. Assurance is what backs it up — the dispute-resolution process the Space runs if a job goes wrong, so both sides have somewhere to turn.

5

Trust Score

Trust Score tracks your behaviour on the Space. The more approved disputes raised against you, the lower it drops — so it's a running read on how reliable you've been to work with.

6

Privacy Policy

Provaspace doesn't use your data for anything shady. You're in safe hands.

What Gets You Banned

As a freelancer, racking up too many disputes raised against you gets you banned. Ban is the more serious action — it locks you out with a blocking overlay on the dashboard.

9

What Gets You Suspended

Suspension kicks in when you owe too much on your Rent. Clear the balance to get back in.

10

What's the Space

The Space is Provaspace itself — where freelancers come to get gigs, and where clients come to find freelancers.

FOR FREELANCERS

Browse and claim gigs posted by clients.

FOR CLIENTS

Post gigs and find freelancers to hire.

11

Withdrawals & Minimum

The minimum withdrawal amount is set by the admin and depends on how the app is progressing — so it can change over time.

12

Referrals

Freelancers

Referrals are a way to get free Rent. Refer someone to the Space — once they complete one gig, you get some Rent credited to you.

  1. Share your referral link with someone new to the Space.
  2. They sign up and complete one gig.
  3. You get free Rent credited to your account.
